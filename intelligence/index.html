The 13 IntelReap intelligence panels

An IntelReap scan is organised into 13 panels. Each panel reports on one part of your connection or device, and each has a deep-dive page that explains every parameter it shows: what is measured, how it is measured and how to interpret a good or a poor result.

Network panels cover your IP address and ISP, the ASN and route your traffic takes, VPN and proxy detection, and live latency, jitter and packet-loss estimates. Device panels cover your operating system, browser, CPU and memory, the graphics stack including WebGL and WebGPU, and the browser features your device supports. Security panels cover HTTPS, WebRTC exposure, permissions, and fingerprint and leak detection. Performance panels report page load timings, Core Web Vitals and rendering benchmarks. The scoreboard combines nine weighted dimensions into one global score, and the live feed logs each action the scanner takes.

These pages explain what each reading means. Your own readings appear when you run the scan on the homepage. For background on the concepts behind the panels, see the learn articles and the guides.
