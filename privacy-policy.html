Privacy Policy

This policy explains what IntelReap processes when you use the tool, which third parties are involved and the choices you have. IntelReap does not require an account and does not sell personal data.

01 Who We Are

IntelReap (intelreap.com) is operated by IntelReap Information Technologies ("IntelReap", "we"). We decide how the limited personal data described in this policy is used. Contact: stmakarios@gmail.com, or use the contact form.

02 What Happens When You Run a Scan

When you open the tool, your browser reads information about your own connection and device using standard browser APIs. The results appear on your screen. To complete the scan, these requests are made:

  • Our backend (hosted on Vercel) receives a request for IP identity and network details. Like any server, it receives your public IP address as part of the connection and uses it to look up your approximate location, ISP and network (ASN) information.
  • A proxy and VPN check sends your time zone, browser language, ASN, network organisation name and country code to our backend.
  • An AI summary request sends scan scores and categories (such as country, ISP, security and performance results) to our backend, which passes them to an AI language-model provider. Before the request leaves your browser, your IP address, city, region, coordinates, local network address, device fingerprint hashes, font and extension lists and request headers are removed.

We do not require an account and do not build user profiles. Our backend processes each request and returns the response; it does not store your scan results.

03 Data That Stays in Your Browser

The following is processed only in your browser and displayed to you. It is not sent to our servers:

  • GPS coordinates, if you grant your browser's location permission
  • Your local network address detected through WebRTC
  • Canvas, audio and font fingerprint values and their hashes
  • Detailed device, graphics and browser capability data

If your browser asks for permission to use your location, you can decline. The tool continues to work.

04 Third-Party Services

IntelReap relies on the following services. Each may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser or our backend contacts it:

  • Netlify: hosts the website and processes contact form submissions
  • Vercel: hosts our backend API
  • IP and network data providers and AI language-model providers: contacted by our backend to return results and summaries
  • Cloudflare (a trace endpoint) and ipify: queried by your browser to detect your public IP address
  • Google STUN server: used by WebRTC to detect your network addresses; Google may see your IP address
  • Stadia Maps: serves map tiles when the location map is shown
  • Google Fonts, unpkg and jsDelivr: serve fonts and JavaScript libraries
  • Google Analytics: used only after you click Accept
  • Google AdSense: used only if advertising is enabled on the site

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

No analytics cookie is set and no request is sent to Google Analytics before you accept. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer. Details are in our Cookies Policy.

07 Contact Form

If you write to us through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message. Submissions are processed through Netlify Forms. We use them only to reply to you and keep them for as long as needed to answer your enquiry and keep a record of it.

We process data to operate and secure the tool (legitimate interests), and we use consent for analytics and advertising cookies. Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you

09 California and US State Privacy Rights

10 International Transfers

Our providers operate in several countries, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses offered by those providers.

11 Retention

IntelReap has no user accounts, and our backend does not store scan results. Our hosting providers (Netlify and Vercel) keep standard request and runtime logs, and the IP data and AI providers we use handle requests under their own retention policies. Analytics data, if you accept, is retained under the retention settings of our Google Analytics property. Contact form messages are kept as described in section 07.

12 Children

IntelReap is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 where local law sets a higher age, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has contacted us, email stmakarios@gmail.com and we will delete the information.
