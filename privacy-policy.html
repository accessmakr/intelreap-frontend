Privacy Policy

IntelReap is built on a single principle: your data belongs to you. Everything this tool does happens inside your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

IntelReap collects zero personal data. All 234 intelligence data points are processed locally in your browser and are never transmitted to any server.

01 Information We Do Not Collect

IntelReap does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personally identifiable information. The following data types are explicitly never collected:

  • IP addresses — detected locally in your browser, never sent to our servers
  • Device fingerprint data — processed client-side and displayed only to you
  • Network scan results — all 234 data points remain on your device
  • Browser capabilities or performance metrics
  • Location coordinates or geolocation data
  • Account information — no accounts exist on IntelReap
  • Usage behaviour, interaction events, or session data
  • Email addresses or any contact information unless voluntarily submitted via the contact form

02 How IntelReap Works

IntelReap is a fully client-side application. When you run an intelligence scan, your browser collects data about your own network connection and device using standard browser APIs. This data is displayed to you in real time and never leaves your device.

The only external communication that occurs is a query to the IntelReap backend API at intelreap-backend.vercel.app to enrich IP geolocation data. This request contains only your public IP address, which is information already visible to any server you connect to on the internet. The response is displayed to you and not stored.

03 Local Storage

IntelReap uses browser localStorage for one purpose only: saving your language preference so the interface loads in your chosen language on your next visit.

  • Key stored: ndic_cookies_accepted — records your cookie notice response
  • Key stored: ndic_language — stores your selected display language

No scan data, no IP addresses, no device information, and no personal identifiers are stored in localStorage. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

04 Cookies

IntelReap does not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party cookies. The only browser storage used is localStorage as described in Section 3 above.

For full details on our storage practices, see the Cookies Policy.

05 Third-Party Services

IntelReap uses the following third-party services in limited capacities:

  • ip-api.com — IP geolocation enrichment. Your public IP is queried to determine approximate location, ISP, and ASN data. ip-api.com's own privacy policy governs this interaction.
  • Google Fonts — Typography loaded from Google's CDN. Google may log font requests per their standard privacy practices.
  • Groq / Gemini — When AI diagnostic summaries are generated, scan summary data is processed by the AI provider to generate the summary text. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

No advertising networks, behavioural analytics platforms, or data brokers receive any information through IntelReap.

06 GDPR Compliance

IntelReap is designed to be fully compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Because we collect no personal data, the majority of GDPR obligations do not apply. Specifically:

  • No lawful basis for data processing is required as no personal data is processed by IntelReap
  • No data subject rights requests can be fulfilled as no data is held
  • No data protection impact assessment is required
  • No data processor agreements are necessary for IntelReap's core operation

EU users can use IntelReap with confidence that their use of the tool creates no GDPR obligations on either party.

07 CCPA Compliance

IntelReap complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Because IntelReap collects no personal information about users, no CCPA disclosure or opt-out mechanism is required. California residents have the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sale of personal information. IntelReap has no personal information to disclose, delete, or sell.

08 Contact Form Data

If you submit a message via the contact form on IntelReap, the name, email address, and message content you provide are transmitted to our form processor (Netlify Forms) solely for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. This data is:

  • Used only to respond to your message
  • Not shared with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes
  • Retained only as long as necessary to address your enquiry
  • Deletable upon request by emailing accessmakr@gmail.com

09 Children's Privacy

IntelReap does not knowingly collect any information from users under the age of 13. The tool does not require any registration and collects no personal information, making it safe for users of all ages.
