How Scanning Works

IntelReap uses standard browser APIs — including the Network Information API, WebRTC, WebGL, the Performance Observer, and navigator hardware properties — to read information your device already exposes to every website you visit. A single lookup of your public IP address is sent for geolocation enrichment; nothing else leaves your browser.

Scoring Methodology

Your Global Intelligence Score weights nine dimensions: network, identity, privacy, device, graphics, security, capability, performance and speed. Security carries the heaviest weight at 20%, reflecting its outsized impact on real-world risk. Scores update live as each panel finishes analysis.
