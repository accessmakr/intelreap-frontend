The 13 panels

What each panel measures and where the data comes from

01Network Route. Who owns the network you reach the internet through, its size tier and the registry that allocated its addresses. Source: MaxMind GeoLite ASN data, plus public network-type lists (ipverse as-metadata, public domain).
02Network Identity. Your public IP address (IPv4 and IPv6 when your device has both), provider and an approximate location. Source: MaxMind GeoLite City data; Cloudflare request details only where that data has no record; OpenStreetMap map tiles.
03VPN and Proxy. Signals that suggest a VPN, proxy or Tor: the network type, time zone and language against the IP location, and the WebRTC address. Source: Rule-based checks written for IntelReap. This is an estimate, not a verdict.
04Live Network. Round-trip time to our server, connection type and stability over time. Source: Timing requests made by your browser and the browser Network Information API.
05Device. Operating system, browser, processor cores, memory and screen. Source: Standard browser APIs read on your device.
06Graphics. Graphics chip and WebGL support. Source: The WebGL API in your browser.
07Security. Whether the page is secure and which exposure settings your browser has. Source: Browser security APIs and checks on this page.
08Capability. Which of 28 modern browser features are available. Source: Feature detection in your browser.
09Performance. Where the time went while this page loaded. Source: The browser Navigation Timing API.
10Rendering Speed. Core Web Vitals and a short benchmark of script, page and graphics work. Source: The browser PerformanceObserver API and a small test run on your device.
11Global Scoreboard. One combined score with nine sub-scores. Source: IntelReap's own formula. It is not an industry standard score.
12Live Feed. A log of events during your scan. Source: Generated by the scan itself.
13Fingerprint and Leaks. How unique your browser looks and whether a local address is exposed. Source: Browser properties and the WebRTC API. The uniqueness score is a heuristic.
Accuracy

What is exact, and what is an estimate

Read directly from your browser, so exact: your device and browser details, graphics support, available features, page timing and the security status of this page.

Estimates, so treat with care:

  • IP location. Databases place an address in a city or region, often a nearby one. Mobile networks and IPv6 addresses are the least precise, and the same phone can appear in different cities on IPv4 and IPv6. The panel shows an accuracy radius for this reason.
  • VPN and proxy detection. It combines several weak signals. A clean result does not prove that no VPN is in use, and a flag can be a false alarm.
  • Fingerprint uniqueness and the scores. These are IntelReap's own calculations meant for comparison between panels, not a certified rating.
  • Live network figures. They depend on your connection at that moment and on the distance to our server.
Freshness

How the reference data is kept current

The location and network-owner databases are refreshed twice a month, on the 1st and the 15th. Network type lists are rebuilt weekly. The written summaries under each panel are produced in your browser from your own results; no scan data is sent to an AI service.

Independence

Funding and editorial independence

IntelReap is an independent project. What the panels report is calculated by the tool; advertising or other placements, where they appear, never change a measurement or a score.

Corrections

Found a mistake?

If a result looks wrong, or an explanation is out of date, tell us through the contact form and include the page and what you saw. We check it, correct it and update the page's "last updated" date. Privacy requests go through the same form.
