Cookies Policy

This page lists what IntelReap stores in your browser, which cookies are set only with your permission and how to change your choice at any time.

Effective 2 October 2026
Last Updated 2 October 2026
Analytics cookies Only with consent

Essential browser storage keeps your language, theme and cookie choice. Google Analytics cookies are set only if you click Accept, and you can withdraw that choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer.

01 What Are Cookies and Local Storage

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device. Local storage is a related browser feature that keeps small pieces of data without sending them to a server with every request. Both can be used for essential functions, preferences, analytics and advertising.

02 What IntelReap Stores

The following items may be stored in your browser:

Name Purpose Expires Set by
ndic_lang Remembers your selected language Until you clear site data IntelReap (local storage)
ndic_theme Remembers your theme preference Until you clear site data IntelReap (local storage)
ndic_analytics_consent Remembers your Accept or Decline choice Until you clear site data or change it IntelReap (local storage)
_ga, _ga_* Distinguish visitors and sessions for Google Analytics. Set only after you click Accept Up to 2 years Google Analytics
Advertising cookies Serve and measure ads, only if advertising is enabled and, where required, after consent Set by Google Google AdSense
Site file cache Copies of IntelReap site files for faster loading and offline use Until the site is updated or you clear site data IntelReap (service worker)

IntelReap does not store your scan results, IP address or fingerprint values in cookies or local storage.

03 Essential and Optional Storage

Language, theme, your cookie choice and the site file cache are essential to how the site works and do not need consent. Google Analytics and advertising cookies are optional and are used only with your consent.

04 Third-Party Cookies

Google Analytics and Google AdSense are operated by Google. Google describes how it uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Personalised advertising can be managed at adssettings.google.com.

05 Managing Your Choices

Click Cookie settings in the footer of any page to accept or decline optional cookies. Declining removes Google Analytics cookies set earlier. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; doing so may reset your language and theme.

06 Changes to This Policy

We update this policy when our use of cookies changes. The date at the top of the page shows the latest revision. Questions: stmakarios@gmail.com.
